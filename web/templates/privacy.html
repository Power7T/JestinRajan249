Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

1. Introduction

HostAI ("we", "us", "our") operates HostAI.app. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

2. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes:

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address, password (hashed), and optional profile information such as property name, location, and contact details.

2.2 Reservation and Guest Data

You may upload reservation data including guest names, phone numbers, email addresses, and check-in/check-out dates. This data is processed to enable draft generation and communication management.

2.3 Communication Data

Messages received from guests through email, WhatsApp, SMS, and other channels are collected, processed, and stored to generate AI drafts and maintain conversation history. You can control data retention through your settings (default: 30 days).

2.4 Usage Data

We collect information on how you interact with the platform (drafts approved/rejected, features used) for analytics, service improvement, and billing purposes.

3. Data Processing and AI

HostAI processes your messages through a third-party AI service (OpenRouter) to generate draft responses. Personal information such as phone numbers and email addresses are redacted before transmission. You can review all AI-generated content before sending.

4. Data Retention

By default, data (activity logs, drafts, timeline events) is retained for 30 days. You can adjust this in your account settings. Subscription data and billing records are retained as required by law.

5. Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your data. When you delete your account, we delete personal information within 30 days, except where required by law. Backup copies may persist for up to 90 days.

6. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to provide functionality:

  • OpenRouter — AI model provider for draft generation
  • Stripe — Payment processing
  • Twilio — SMS and voice delivery (if enabled)
  • Meta Cloud API — WhatsApp integration (if enabled)
  • Deepgram — Voice transcription (if voice enabled)

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on computers located outside your state, province, country, or other governmental jurisdiction where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@hostai.app.

10. Children's Privacy

HostAI is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 18.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date at the top.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@hostai.app.
