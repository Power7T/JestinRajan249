Step 2 — Connect your calendar

last step

Paste your Airbnb iCal link so HostAI knows your reservations. That's it — you're live.

Paste one URL per line if you have multiple properties.

📍 How to find your Airbnb iCal URL
  1. Open Airbnb and go to your listing
  2. Click Calendar at the top
  3. Click Availability settings (sidebar)
  4. Scroll down to Sync calendars
  5. Click Export Calendar and copy the link
  6. Paste it above — that's it

Don't have an Airbnb listing yet? Skip this step and add it later from Settings.

⚠ iCal URL not working? Try these:

  1. Make sure you copied the full URL — it should start with https://www.airbnb.com/calendar/ical/
  2. The URL must end in .ics
  3. In Airbnb, go to your listing → Calendar → Availability settings → scroll to Sync calendars → click Export Calendar
  4. Do not use the "Import" URL — only the Export link works
  5. If you just generated the link, wait 30 seconds and try again
  6. On mobile? Copy the full link by long-pressing on it

Still not working? Skip this step and add the calendar from Settings later.
