Frequently asked questions

What happens after my free trial ends? add

Your account switches to the Free plan automatically — no charge and no interruption to your web dashboard. You only get billed if you choose to stay on a paid plan.

Can I change plans later? add

Yes — you can upgrade or downgrade any time from your Billing settings. Changes take effect immediately and you'll be pro-rated on the next invoice.

What's the difference between Baileys and Meta Cloud API? add

Baileys routes WhatsApp through a local node.js process running on your own PC — great for personal numbers, but requires a machine online 24/7. Meta Cloud API is the official WhatsApp Business solution: no hardware required, scales to thousands of messages per day, and is the more reliable choice for serious hosts.

Do I need to bring my own Twilio / Meta credentials? add

Yes. HostAI connects to your own Twilio or Meta Business account so you keep full ownership and control of your phone numbers and conversation history.

Is my guest data safe? add

Guest messages are processed through OpenRouter with PII (phone numbers, emails) redacted before leaving our servers. You control data retention in Settings — default is 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
