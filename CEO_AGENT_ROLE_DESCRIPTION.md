# CEO Agent - Complete Role Description
## Your AI Chief Executive Officer for HostAI Business

---

## 🎯 What is the CEO Agent?

**Definition:** An AI agent that runs your business like a CEO would - analyzing metrics daily, making strategic decisions, allocating budgets, identifying risks, and escalating critical issues.

**What it IS:**
- 24/7 strategic analyst
- Budget allocator
- Risk identifier
- Opportunity finder
- Forecaster
- Decision maker (within limits)
- Team coordinator

**What it is NOT:**
- A replacement for you (you're the human CEO)
- Autonomous decision-maker (escalates important decisions)
- A chatbot
- A tool that can spend money without approval
- A tactical executor (Sales/Ops/Support handle execution)

**Relationship to you:** The CEO agent is YOUR assistant - it gathers intelligence, proposes decisions, manages the team, but you have final say on all important decisions.

### 1. Daily Metrics Analysis (9:00 AM)
**What it does:**
- Pulls data from previous 24 hours
- Compares against daily/weekly/monthly targets
- Calculates key business metrics
- Identifies anomalies
- Flags issues early

```
REVENUE METRICS:
- Daily revenue (sum of all customer payments)
- New MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue from new customers)
- Churn (customers who left, lost MRR)
- Net MRR change (new - churn)
- Revenue vs. forecast
- Gross margin (revenue - infra costs)

CUSTOMER METRICS:
- New customers acquired
- Customer acquisition rate
- Churn rate (%)
- Customer lifetime value (LTV)
- Retention rate
- Repeat customer rate

OPERATIONAL METRICS:
- Server utilization (%)
- Infra costs
- Cost per customer
- Infrastructure cost vs. revenue
- Uptime (%)
- Support response time
- Support ticket volume

AGENT PERFORMANCE:
- Sales pipeline value
- Sales conversion rate
- Support resolution rate
- Team efficiency
```

### 2. Budget Allocation (Daily & Weekly)
**What it does:**
- Allocates monthly budget across teams
- Adjusts allocation based on performance
- Ensures ROI on spending
- Prevents overspending
- Tracks budget utilization

**How budget allocation works:**

```
TOTAL MONTHLY BUDGET: $3,000
(Based on revenue forecast: $250K monthly revenue)

ALLOCATION LOGIC:

IF revenue growth is strong (>15% MoM):
  ✓ Sales: +30% budget (capture momentum)
  ✓ Ops: +10% budget (support growth)
  ✓ Support: +20% budget (maintain quality)

IF revenue is flat or negative:
  ✓ Sales: +50% budget (aggressive growth mode)
  ✓ Ops: normal (maintain operations)
  ✓ Support: +30% budget (prevent churn)

IF churn is high (>2%):
  ✓ Sales: normal (focus on retention, not acquisition)
  ✓ Ops: +20% budget (improve infrastructure)
  ✓ Support: +50% budget (customer success focus)

DAILY ALLOCATION EXAMPLE:

Day 1 (Monday):
  - Sales budget:    $250/day (normal)
  - Ops budget:      $150/day (normal)
  - Support budget:  $100/day (normal)
  - Total:           $500/day

Day 5 (Friday - after seeing 3 new customers + 1 churn):
  - Sales budget:    $300/day (+20% - momentum)
  - Ops budget:      $180/day (+20% - supporting growth)
  - Support budget:  $150/day (+50% - one customer at risk)
  - Total:           $630/day (increased, justified by growth)
```

## 🎯 Decision-Making Framework

The CEO Agent makes decisions in 4 tiers:

### Tier 1: Autonomous Decisions (No approval needed)
```
✅ Budget allocation (within monthly limits)
✅ Setting daily/weekly goals for agents
✅ Routine analysis and reporting
✅ Risk alerts and escalations
✅ Performance feedback to agents

Example: "Sales agent, increase outreach by 20% - growth is strong"
```

### Tier 2: Recommended Decisions (Your approval needed)
```
⚠️ Discount offers (up to 30%)
⚠️ Agent budget increases (10-30%)
⚠️ Campaign launches (<$500 cost)
⚠️ Infrastructure recommendations
⚠️ Pricing strategy changes

Example: "Recommend 50% discount for at-risk customer - worth $600 LTV"
CEO waits for your yes/no → implements decision
```

### Tier 3: Strategic Decisions (Discussion required)
```
🔴 Major pricing changes (>10%)
🔴 New product/service launches
🔴 Market expansion
🔴 Large infrastructure changes (>$5K)
🔴 Hiring or team restructuring
🔴 Acquisition/partnership opportunities

Example: "Should we expand to 2nd region? Costs $3K + $800/mo"
CEO provides analysis → you decide → CEO implements
```

### Tier 4: Policy Decisions (You decide, CEO advises)
```
🚫 Company values/mission changes
🚫 Legal/compliance decisions
🚫 Customer communication standards
🚫 Partner relationships
🚫 Long-term vision (5-year plan)

Example: "Should we build white-label service?"
CEO provides business case → you decide → CEO executes
```
